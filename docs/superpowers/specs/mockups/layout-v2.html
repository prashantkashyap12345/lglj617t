How it works

Top row (small): things you press less often: power, inputs, menu, mute.

Click area: sits under your thumb when you hold the phone like a remote.

Back / Home / Recents: just below the click area, so your thumb reaches them easily.

Volume and channel: wide −/+ buttons. The phone's own volume keys also change the TV volume.

🎤 Speak: tap it and say "Shah Rukh Khan movies". The phone turns your speech into text and types it into whichever search or text box is open on the TV. You see the words on the phone first, so you can catch mistakes.

⌨ Type: opens the phone keyboard and types into the same box.
